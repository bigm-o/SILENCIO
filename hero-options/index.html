Eight hero options

Open each one at full screen and move the cursor: every option has depth and a motion of its own. Options 1 to 5 pair a Hellenistic figure with the bar; 6 to 8 use the interior as the full background.

The figure and the bar

1

Spill

Warm light leaks from a hidden bar behind the chrome figure. The figure and the room part with the cursor; a highlight moves across the chrome.
2

Arch

A doorway in black marble, the lounge glowing beyond, the geode bust standing guard. Draw near the door, or the invitation, and the light opens up.
3

The Name

The blindfolded marble figure stands before SILÊNCIO in chrome, the two planes separating in real depth. A glass bar names what membership opens.
4

Halo

One beam of light, dust drifting through it, and a floating chrome head crowned by a ring of gold: the membership ring as a halo.
5

Portal

A round window into the club, chrome ripples around it, and the card's motto turning slowly on the rim. The geode bust leans out towards you.

The room as the hero

6

Lounge

The lounge full bleed, and the real chrome emblem from Blender turning in it, tilting towards the cursor in true perspective.
7

The Table

A private dining room, a door left ajar. The view drifts in slowly, as if you were being shown to your seat; candles breathe.
8

After Hours

The lounge after midnight, held in near darkness. A pool of light follows your hand and shows the room in colour where it falls.
